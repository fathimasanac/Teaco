FICO Tea is a black tea powder designed for everyday tea preparation and commercial food-service use. It provides a familiar, strong tea character suitable for regular tea service across cafés, tea shops, restaurants, cafeterias, and other businesses.

Available in a 5 KG commercial pack, FICO Tea is a practical choice for businesses looking for a dependable everyday black tea option for regular-volume service.
